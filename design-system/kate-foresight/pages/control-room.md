# Control room (overrides MASTER)
- Desktop operator workspace, max width 1280px, compact header (demo date, cohort size, refresh state).
- Five tabs with `role="tablist"`: Overview, Moments, Advisor queue, Rule studio, Trust & audit. Selected tab and filters persist in the URL hash.
- Overview: 4 metric tiles (each with a one-line definition), 2 charts (moments by type, channel distribution), a short attention list. Fits 1440x900 without scrolling the tiles and charts.
- Tables: 14px text, zebra-free, 1px row lines, sticky header, pagination "1-25 of 412".
- Drawers for row details (right side panel, same component as the customer Why panel).
